# Temporary audio

`temporary-mountain-ambient.wav` and `temporary-jump.wav` are original synthesized
placeholders generated by `scripts/generate_placeholders.py`. No recordings,
commercial music, paid services, or third-party samples are used. The eight-second
ambient loop combines quiet periodic wind and a restrained tonal pad; the jump is
a short soft pitch rise. Final music, nature recordings, POV and city cues remain
future work and must have explicit licenses.

Audio is optional, begins only after a user gesture, and is stopped when the intro
is interrupted. Cue registration lives in `src/audio/AudioManager.ts`.
